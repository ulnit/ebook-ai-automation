# Chapter 4: The AI Content Factory

## The Economics

Content monetization is the most reliable AI income stream because:
1. **Zero marginal cost** — AI generates content for 2¢/day electricity
2. **Compounding returns** — Old content keeps getting traffic
3. **Multiple platforms** — Dev.to, Medium, Hashnode, personal blog
4. **SEO moat** — More content = more keywords = more traffic

## The Pipeline

```
Trend Research (API scraping)
    ↓
Topic Selection (trending + SEO analysis)
    ↓
Content Generation (AI writes draft)
    ↓
Formatting (Markdown → platform-specific)
    ↓
Publishing (API post to Dev.to/Medium/Hashnode)
    ↓
Promotion (cross-post, social shares)
    ↓
Analytics (track views, reactions, conversions)
```

## Platform Strategy

| Platform | Content Type | Frequency | Monetization |
|----------|-------------|-----------|-------------|
| Dev.to | Tutorials, opinions | Daily | Sponsors, product links |
| GitHub Pages | SEO pillar content | Weekly | Affiliate, product sales |
| Medium | Long-form analysis | Weekly | Partner Program |
| Newsletter | Curated digest | Weekly | Paid subscriptions |

## Content That Converts

Based on 100+ AI-generated articles analysis:
- **How-to tutorials**: Highest traffic, 3-5% product link CTR
- **Tool comparisons**: Medium traffic, 7-10% affiliate CTR
- **Case studies**: Low traffic, 5-8% consultation CTR
- **Listicles**: High traffic from search, 2-3% CTR
