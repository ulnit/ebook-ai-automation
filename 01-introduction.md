# Chapter 1: Why Automate?

## The $35 AI Employee

In 2026, you can hire an AI agent for $35 — once. Not per month. Once. It runs 24/7, doesn't take breaks, and costs 2 cents per day in electricity.

This book is about that agent. How to build it, train it, and most importantly — how to make it earn money.

## What You'll Learn

- Setting up an autonomous AI agent on a Raspberry Pi
- Building automated income streams
- Content generation at scale
- Bug bounty hunting automation
- Digital product creation and sales

## Who This Is For

Developers who want to build systems that make money while they sleep. No ML expertise required — just Python and curiosity.
